# Sample Review

## Overall Assessment

The draft has a promising topic but currently reads as a broad overview. Its main priority is to turn the topic into an answerable research question and then build the literature review around the debate that question enters.

## Major Issues

1. The introduction delays the research question until the end of page two. Move the question and thesis into the opening section.
2. The literature review summarizes authors individually. Reorganize it around two debates: whether digitization expands access and whether it reproduces administrative burden.
3. The methodology section names interviews but does not explain sampling, interview count, recruitment, or coding procedure.

## Suggested Revision

Frame the paper around this question: "How do digital welfare systems change the administrative burdens experienced by applicants?" Then use the analysis sections to examine learning costs, compliance costs, and psychological costs.
