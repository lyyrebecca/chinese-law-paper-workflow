# Good Outline

## Title

Digital Welfare Platforms and Administrative Burden in Urban China

## Research Question

How do digital welfare application systems reshape low-income residents' access to public assistance in urban China?

## Thesis

Digital platforms reduce some transaction costs but create new administrative burdens through documentation demands, interface literacy requirements, and discretionary gatekeeping by street-level officials.

## Structure

1. Introduction: presents the puzzle that digitization promises access while applicants report exclusion.
2. Literature review: synthesizes work on administrative burden, digital governance, and welfare access.
3. Theory: defines learning, compliance, and psychological costs in digital welfare encounters.
4. Methodology: explains interview sample, policy document analysis, and limits of city-level generalization.
5. Analysis I: shows how documentation requirements shift costs onto applicants.
6. Analysis II: examines how digital interfaces privilege applicants with institutional knowledge.
7. Analysis III: explains how local officials mediate platform rules.
8. Conclusion: argues that digitization changes the location of discretion rather than eliminating it.
