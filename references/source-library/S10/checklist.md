# Social Science Paper Checklist

## Argument

- The paper has a clear, answerable research question.
- The thesis directly answers the research question.
- The introduction explains the puzzle, stakes, contribution, and roadmap.
- Each major section advances the thesis rather than merely adding information.
- Key concepts are defined before they are used.

## Literature

- The literature review is organized by debate, mechanism, concept, method, or case.
- Sources are synthesized rather than summarized one by one.
- The paper identifies a gap, tension, limitation, or unresolved question.
- The contribution follows logically from the literature review.

## Method and Evidence

- The paper explains data sources, case selection, sampling, and scope.
- Methods are appropriate to the question and claims.
- Evidence is interpreted, not just presented.
- Limitations and validity concerns are acknowledged.

## Style and Citations

- Topic sentences state analytic claims.
- Paragraphs have one main job.
- Citations support specific claims and are formatted consistently.
- Quotations are used sparingly and interpreted after being introduced.
- The conclusion returns to the argument and explains its broader implication.
