# Revision Workflow

## First Pass: Argument

- Locate the research question and thesis.
- Check whether every section answers the question.
- Remove or reframe sections that only provide background.
- Strengthen the introduction and conclusion after revising the body.

## Second Pass: Structure

- Give each section a clear job.
- Make topic sentences argumentative.
- Add transitions that explain logical movement, not just sequence.
- Move literature, theory, method, and evidence into the section where each does the most work.

## Third Pass: Evidence

- Mark every major claim and verify support.
- Add citations where scholarly or factual support is missing.
- Explain tables, figures, quotes, or examples in prose.
- Narrow claims that outrun the evidence.

## Fourth Pass: Style

- Replace vague nouns with specific concepts.
- Cut throat-clearing phrases.
- Prefer active verbs and precise causal or interpretive language.
- Check citation style, headings, formatting, and reference list consistency.
